# Kaveri Electronic Halaguru • Customer Membership Privacy Notice
**Prepared under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**

---

### Notice to Members & Customers:
This notice is provided by **Kaveri Electronic Halaguru** (HMS Complex, Kanakapura Main Road, Halaguru Hobli, Karnataka - 571421) in its capacity as a **Data Fiduciary**.

### 1. What Information Do We Collect?
When you join the Kaveri Member Club, we collect:
- **Full Name** (Mandatory): To personalize your digital membership card and store warranty records.
- **Primary Mobile Number** (Mandatory): To issue your unique sequential Membership ID (e.g. `KAV-000001`) and authenticate your identity.
- **Alternative Mobile & Email** (Optional): As secondary contact channels if provided.
- **Address / Area** (Optional): To verify appliance delivery feasibility.

### 2. Why Do We Process This Information?
- To grant you membership benefits and track transparent loyalty reward points.
- To link your in-store purchases with manufacturer warranty protection.
- To offer customer service and product repair updates when requested.

### 3. Promotional Communication is Voluntary & Separate
- Receiving promotional offers via **WhatsApp** or **SMS** requires your explicit, affirmative opt-in.
- Marketing checkboxes are unchecked by default during registration.
- Agreeing to promotional messages is completely optional and is not required to join the club, earn loyalty points, or purchase products.

### 4. How Can You Exercise Your Rights?
Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:
1. **Withdraw Consent (Opt-Out)**: You can withdraw marketing consent at any time through our online Customer Preferences Portal (`/#preferences`). Your opt-out takes effect immediately.
2. **Review & Correct Your Data**: You can view your registered details and request corrections through our portal or by visiting our store counter in HMS Complex.
3. **Account Closure & Erasure**: You can request closure of your membership account and erasure of personal data, subject only to statutory tax and invoice retention obligations.
